How diplomacy works

All times are game time: they stop while the game is paused and run faster at 3x. Peace is permanent; after a treaty a {{ truceLabel }} truce forbids war declarations. War exhaustion above 25 cuts credit, raw material and research output (up to 30%) and resets when peace is signed. You also win if every surviving empire is at peace with you, no rebel planet remains and you hold more than half of the inhabited planets.
